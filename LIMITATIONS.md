# Reading Brontide responsibly

Brontide is a visual exploration tool built from a frozen arXiv snapshot.

- **Coverage:** January 2007–June 2026; the last year is partial. arXiv is a
  changing subset of scholarship, so its field shares do not measure all science.
- **Sampling:** 149,994 displayed papers represent a stratified sample. Monthly
  statistics use aggregate counts. Density measures the display sample.
- **Geometry:** the projection and groups use the full frozen corpus. Earlier
  windows are retrospective; proximity and gaps are not calibrated measures.
- **Currents:** ribbon thickness is a trailing-year publication share. The layout
  does not depict citation links, transfers or influence between fields.
- **Forecasts:** the archived experiment begins in June 2026. Its independent
  regional forecasts are not jointly normalized and have no displayed calibrated
  intervals. The selection pipeline beat its comparator at **0 of 3** cutoffs;
  known validation defects limit what that result establishes.

See [data, methods and forecast scope](docs/DATA.md) for the quantities behind
these views and [the frozen evaluation](artifacts/validation.md) for its record.
